A dreamlike camping spot with rolling green meadows, seasonal flowers, and trekking through the mystical valley.

Spiti Valley ⭐ 4.8

A rugged high-altitude desert offering surreal landscapes, ancient monasteries, and camping under a sky full of stars.

Tirthan Valley ⭐ 4.7

A hidden paradise with riverside camping, trout fishing, and eco-friendly stays inside the Great Himalayan National Park.
